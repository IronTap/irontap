When torn between two values, RIR now accepts a range: tapping the button next to the selected one gives 2-3, 4-5+… (two consecutive values). The range shows as “RIR 2-3” in the exercise history and workout details, can be typed as “2-3” when editing, and is included in exports (“2 à 3” in the CSV, so spreadsheets don’t read it as a date). RIR values already logged stay as they are.
